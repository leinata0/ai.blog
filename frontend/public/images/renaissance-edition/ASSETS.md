# Renaissance Edition — original scene assets

Created 2026-10-23 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These are fictional editorial scene elements, not news photographs. Shopify Winter ’26 was used as a visual and interaction reference; no Shopify artwork, branding or source code was copied.

- `world.jpg`: 1672 × 941, environment layer. Converted from the original generated PNG to JPEG (quality 89); no scene edits.
- `figures.png`: 1672 × 941, RGBA foreground. The two figures are drawn independently from the left and right portions of this same texture, preserving their common lighting and composition.
- The foreground ribbon, reflections, geometry deformation, camera motion and dissolve are rendered by code. They are not baked into these images.
- Originals remain under the local Codex generated-images directory. Runtime assets live here so they are included in the project.

## Final environment prompt

Create an original immersive website environment artwork, panoramic 16:9 composition, exceptionally polished cinematic Renaissance oil painting crossed with modern science-fiction art direction. A lush deep emerald garden on floating ochre limestone terraces, sculpted dark cypress trees framing the far left and right edges, a distant pale-blue lake and impossible classical arch in the center distance, rich cobalt and muted lilac sky, monumental sunlit cumulus clouds, cream peonies and dark foliage along the lower edges. Late afternoon amber light from upper left, atmospheric aerial depth, tactile brushwork and refined photographic detail, sophisticated exhibition art rather than fantasy game concept art. The entire center 35 percent should be calm open sky and a distant low horizon, suitable for overlaying a narrow editorial title panel. Tall trees remain mainly at outer edges; foreground corners have lush detailed vegetation. Environment only, absolutely no people, no statues, no letters, no text, no logos, no UI, no gadgets, no glowing circuits. This is a separate environment layer to be animated with foreground people, so it must already fill the whole frame beautifully, with vivid emerald, ochre, cream and pale azure color relationships; not a dark neon background.

## Final figure prompt

Create a large original isolated foreground artwork for an immersive interactive Renaissance-inspired AI editorial website. Wide 16:9 TRANSPARENT canvas showing TWO separate fully clothed adult human figures in the dramatic tactile style of an extremely refined Renaissance oil painting, with exquisite faces, sculptural folds, believable hands, rich warm/cool lighting. On the LEFT third, an elegant dark-haired woman in flowing ivory silk and gold-lined drapery floats diagonally inward, torso leaning toward the center, one open hand extended gently to the right; her ivory sleeves and skirt billow in sculptural curves and full figure is visible. On the RIGHT third, a thoughtful dark-haired man in dark emerald tunic and a voluminous copper-red cloak floats diagonally inward, one hand extended to the left, other arm curving outward, drapery and legs fully visible. Both face each other, their hands almost meet across a large empty central gap. Leave the middle 26 percent completely transparent to accommodate a narrow central HTML title panel, with NO overlap between the two figures across the central dividing line. Figures occupy about 85 percent of canvas height and should feel monumental, graceful, editorial and artistic rather than stock photo or videogame characters. Warm afternoon light from upper left, reflected cool pale-blue light from below. No scenery, no ground plane, no shadows cast onto a background, no framing, no pedestal, no words, no logos, no devices, no graphic UI. Fine natural flyaway hair and crisp fully transparent silhouette edges. Actual transparent alpha background.
